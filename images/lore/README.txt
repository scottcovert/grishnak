DROP LORE PLATES HERE
=====================

Filename: lore-<n>.jpg   where <n> is the Stonebreaker WALL NUMBER, 1 to 29.
  e.g. lore-1.jpg  appears after you clear wall 1
       lore-14.jpg appears after you clear wall 14

Size:   1280 x 864  (2x the game canvas)
Format: JPG preferred (PNG also works; JPG is far smaller for painted work)
Style:  same painted style as the title splash screens, but darker and quieter

The image REPLACES the words: it fills the frame edge to edge, with only a
dim "WALL n" and "SPACE" on a thin strip along the bottom. No sentence.
A missing image is NOT a gap -- that wall just shows its written line the way
it always did. So the words are the fallback and the painting is the upgrade.
Any subset, in any order, is fine.

Keep nothing important in the bottom ~30px (the labels sit there).
No readable sentences inside the picture.

NO LEADING ZEROS: lore-3.jpg, never lore-03.jpg. All lower case.
The NUMBER decides WHERE the picture plays, so pick it to suit the mood:
  walls 1-8   the valley as it was  (cozy, sunlit)
  walls 9-17  something is off      (quiet wrongness)
  walls 18-25 it is bad now         (dark, figures, power)
  wall  26    the reveal            (the toy)
  walls 27-29 after the reveal      (quieter, matter-of-fact)

Wall 30 is THE WARLORD and has NO slot -- clearing him plays THE OTHER FACE.
(27-29 were added 2026-08-21. Nothing below 26 moved.)

NOT THE SAME as the title paintings, which go one folder UP:
  images/splash-6.jpg .. splash-10.jpg
